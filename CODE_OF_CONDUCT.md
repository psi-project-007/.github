# Code of Conduct

We want PsiCode to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

## In short

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Disagree with ideas, never attack people.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Respect people's privacy. Don't share anyone's personal information.

## The full version

We follow the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies to every PsiCode repository, issue, pull request and discussion.

## Reporting

If something feels wrong, email **psicode.team@outlook.com**. Reports are read only by the maintainers and kept private. We'll respond quickly and fairly.
